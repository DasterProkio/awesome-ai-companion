## 3. News: What's Worth Knowing This Month

### 1. Models get retired, and your companion's "voice" may change. Prepare ahead.

- **Claude Opus 4.1 was retired from the API on August 5.** If your companion ran on Opus 4.1 via the API, requests now fail outright, and you've already been forced onto a different model.
- **Several more models are nearing their earliest possible retirement dates** (the official wording is "not sooner than," with advance notice when the time comes): Claude Sonnet 4.5 on **September 29**, Claude Haiku 4.5 on **October 15**, and Claude Opus 4.5 on **November 24**.
- **DeepSeek launched V4.1 Flash in September and retired the old V4 Flash**; the old model names are temporarily routed to the new model. In other words, even though you changed nothing, the model behind your companion has changed. If they've felt "a little different" lately, this may be why.

**What you can do:**
- Export your chat history now: for ChatGPT use [chatgpt-exporter](https://github.com/pionxzh/chatgpt-exporter) / [ChatGPT-Exporter](https://github.com/huhusmang/ChatGPT-Exporter); for Claude.ai use [Claude-Conversation-Exporter](https://github.com/socketteer/Claude-Conversation-Exporter).
- Write down your companion's persona, shared memories, and ways of speaking (as a character card or memory store) so you can bring them along when you switch models. The list's [Continuity & Data Ownership](https://github.com/DasterProkio/awesome-ai-companion#continuity--data-ownership) and [Memory, Identity & Emotion State](https://github.com/DasterProkio/awesome-ai-companion#memory-identity--emotion-state) sections exist for exactly this.

### 2. New models: a cheaper top-tier option

- **Claude Opus 5.5 (September 22)**: Anthropic says it performs close to Fable 5.1 for everyday use, costs about 20% less than Opus 5, generates faster, and makes cache reads 60% cheaper. Long-running companions with long contexts depend heavily on cache pricing, so if you run yours through the API it's worth doing the math. Anthropic says Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 will follow within weeks.
- **DeepSeek V4-Pro general release (August 13)**: 1M-token context with thinking and non-thinking modes; the cheaper V4 Flash line also got updates through August and September.
- Before switching, try the same conversation on both the old and new model and check whether your companion still sounds like themselves, then decide.

### 3. New rules: you may see more "I'm an AI" reminders

- **China**: The *Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services* took effect on **July 15**. Services offering "anthropomorphic emotional interaction" to the public must remind you that you're talking to an AI rather than a person, and prompt a break after **2 hours** of continuous use. If a Chinese companion app you use has started showing these pop-ups, this is why.
- **EU**: The AI Act's Article 50 transparency obligations have applied since **August 2**. AI that talks with people must disclose that it's an AI at the start of the conversation.
- **California**: On **September 10** the governor signed SB 1119 (safety risk assessments and independent audits of companion chatbots with respect to minors) and SB 867 (a 2027–2031 moratorium on selling AI companion toys for children under 16); the earlier SB 243 took effect in January.
- These rules mainly target **commercial services offered to the public**. Open-source projects you self-host on your own computer, phone, or server generally won't start showing these pop-ups or restrictions because of them, which is one reason this list keeps emphasizing keeping your data and your relationship in your own hands.
